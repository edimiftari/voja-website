About Voja Studio

Before the renders and the boxes, there was architecture school.

Voja Studio is led by Edi Miftari, creative director, with a degree in architecture and years of work across 3D visualisation, motion design, packaging, branding and graphic design.

That architectural background brings a builder's discipline to the work. Each project is planned around how it will be made and used, not only how it will look.

From shell to shine

How a project runs

  1. Brief

    A call to understand the product, the audience and where the work will live.

  2. Concept

    Sketches, references and a first block-out so the direction is agreed early.

  3. Production

    Modelling, design and animation, with review rounds at fixed points.

  4. Delivery

    Final renders, films or print-ready files in every format you need.
